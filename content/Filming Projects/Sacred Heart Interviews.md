## Interviewee: Father Mackin
1. Father Mackin, thank you for sharing your extensive knowledge with us. As we celebrate the 350th anniversary of the Sacred Heart apparitions, could you explain the central theological significance of the Sacred Heart of Jesus? What core truths does this devotion invite us to contemplate?
2. For those who may not be familiar, could you briefly describe the historical context of the apparitions to St. Margaret Mary Alacoque and why they were so pivotal for the Church?
3. What are some of the key promises or messages associated with the Sacred Heart devotion, and how do these promises continue to resonate with Catholics today?
4. Is there a particular aspect of the Sacred Heart devotion that you find especially meaningful in your own priestly life?
5. How has the parish prepared for this event?
6. What spiritual fruits do you hope this 350th-anniversary celebration will yield for our parish community and beyond?
