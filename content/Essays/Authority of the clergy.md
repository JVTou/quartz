You should not make a distinction between normal and crisis times on how to approach the authority of the clergy. You can't start drawing the line at external factors, since the role of the priest is independent of the state of the Church (C.S. Lewis, Learning in Wartime)

# The Priest is a Figure of Christ

## Their Role Face to Face

What is the role of the priest? When do we ask priests for advice? For our spiritual needs. But are priests just spiritual therapy? Families should rely upon their head for a unified spiritual path. Priests are the figures of Christ on earth, the spiritual head of their parishioners. As such, they are also responsible for the well being of their parish, and need obedience from their parishioners.

## The Religious Figure

Having an abundance of priests never discounts one from establishing an independent thought. Over reliance on priests is analogous to becoming complacent in matters of the faith. The priest is a figure of Christ, and our relationship with the priest should not distract from our relationship with Him.
