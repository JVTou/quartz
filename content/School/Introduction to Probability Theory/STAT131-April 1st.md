Experiment: any process whose outcomes are pre-identified. E.g:

- Coin flip, outcomes are {$heads, tails$}
- Die toss, outcomes are {$1,2,3,4,5,6$}
Sample Space: collection of all potential outcomes $\Omega$. E.g:
- Human height, possible values are {4-8 ft.}
Events: an event is a subset of $\Omega$, $\omega$: $\Omega$={$\omega_{1},\omega_{2},\omega_{3},\dots$}. E.g:
- $E_{1}=${$\omega_{1},\omega_{2}$}
- $E_{1}=${even numbers}={$2,4,6$}
