$$
e_{ss}=\lim_{ s \to 0 } s\frac{1}{1+\frac{G_{\text{closed loop}}(s)}{s^n}} \frac{1}{s^{k+1}}
$$

With:

- $k$ the order of the disturbance ($k=0$: step, $k=1$: ramp, etc.)
- $n$ the number of poles at the origin
