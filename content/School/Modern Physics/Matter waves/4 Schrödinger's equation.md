We previously derived [[3 Schrödinger's equation without external forces]], the equation that a matter wave $\Psi(x,t)$ obeys if no interactions or external forces are acting on it.

With external forces, Schrödinger's equation generalizes to:

$$
-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\frac{ \partial^{2} \Psi(x,t) }{ \partial x^{2} } +U(x)\Psi(x,t)=i\hbar \frac{ \partial \Psi(x,t) }{ \partial t } 
$$

To determine the behavior of a particle in quantum mechanics is equivalent to solving Schrödinger's equation for $\Psi(x,t)$, given a potential $U(x)$.
