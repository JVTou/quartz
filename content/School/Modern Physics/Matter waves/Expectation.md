# Expectation

The expectation value of a specific quantity means the average value we will get after multiple measurements of that quantity over the entire domain of the system. For a quantity $Q$, the expectation, noted $\langle Q\rangle$, is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle Q\rangle=\int\psi^*(x)\widehat{Q}\psi(x)dx
\end{aligned}
$$

$\widehat{Q}$ is called the quantum mechanical operator representing the physical quantity Q. We apply the operator to a function to obtain Q. Some examples of operators are:

-   The kinetic energy operator $\widehat{K}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}$
-   The total energy operator $\widehat{E}=i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}$
