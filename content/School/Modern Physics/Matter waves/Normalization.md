# Normalization

Normalization is fine-tuning the amplitude of the wave-function so that $\psi_{max}$ is 1. Concretely, it means finding $\psi_{max}$ such that:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int|\psi(x,t)|^2dx=1
\end{aligned}
$$

This is done while taking the integral over the entire domain of the wave-function.
