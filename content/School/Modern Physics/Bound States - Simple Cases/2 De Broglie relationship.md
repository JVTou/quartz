## De Broglie Relationship

To change energy states, the particle must absorb a photon of a certain wavelength given by the de Broglie relationship:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\text{E}=\hbar\omega=\hbar \frac{c}{\lambda}
\end{aligned}
$$

To know $\Delta E$ in an H-like atom, it's useful to know [[Total energy of an electron in an H-like atom]].
